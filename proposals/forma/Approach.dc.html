Section 01

Approach

Sound is written before it is recorded. We agree the sonic world in words and references first, so every session that follows has a target. Nothing is improvised into the final mix.

Listen

Brief, picture, references and the world the brand already lives in. We map what the piece is asking for emotionally before proposing a single instrument.

Direction

A written sonic direction: palette, instrumentation, tonal centre, dynamic arc, reference playlist. Signed off before recording begins, so the creative risk sits at the cheapest point in the process.

Build

Composition, arrangement and sound design against picture. Organic instrumentation recorded in the room, processed only as far as the direction allows. Review cuts at each agreed checkpoint.

Deliver

Mix, master and delivery in every format the campaign needs. Stems and session files handed over so the work stays usable long after the launch.

Working principle

Organic enough to feel viscerally real. Ethereal enough to evoke dream logic. The balance is the craft.
